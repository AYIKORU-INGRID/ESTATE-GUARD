Verification & Safety

Verify a property before you commit money or personal details. A genuine listing should withstand clear questions and independent checks.

Check the Property

  • Visit in person during daylight.
  • Confirm the exact address and boundaries.
  • Compare the property with its advertisement.

Check the Seller

  • Ask for formal identification.
  • Confirm authority to rent or sell.
  • Verify ownership records independently.

Protect Your Money

  • Never send deposits before verification.
  • Use traceable payment methods.
  • Keep all receipts and written agreements.

What verification does not do: checking a listing lowers the risk but does not guarantee that a property is safe, still available or correctly priced. Always verify for yourself before you pay.

Warning Signs of a Fake Broker or Listing

  • They ask for a deposit or full payment before you have seen the place.
  • They pressure you to pay today because someone else is coming.
  • The price is far lower than similar places nearby.
  • They cannot show proof that they own or manage the property.
  • They avoid meeting in person and only use calls or WhatsApp.
  • They refuse to give a written agreement or a receipt.
