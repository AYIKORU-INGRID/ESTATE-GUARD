Verification & Safety

Verify a property before you commit money or personal details. A genuine listing should withstand clear questions and independent checks.

Check the Property

  • Visit in person during daylight.
  • Confirm the exact address and boundaries.
  • Compare the property with its advertisement.

Check the Seller

  • Ask for formal identification.
  • Confirm authority to rent or sell.
  • Verify ownership records independently.

Protect Your Money

  • Never send deposits before verification.
  • Use traceable payment methods.
  • Keep all receipts and written agreements.
